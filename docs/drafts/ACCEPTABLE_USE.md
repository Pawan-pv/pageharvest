# PageHarvest Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft for legal review. Not in force.** Have a lawyer review this text, together with the [Terms of Use](TERMS_OF_USE.md), before it is published or linked from the store listing.

You may use PageHarvest to collect information you are entitled to access and to use. You must not use PageHarvest to:

- collect from a site whose terms or technical measures forbid it, or after the site has asked you to stop;
- get around logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits or other access controls, or collect content you are not authorised to see;
- collect personal data about people, such as names, contact details or profiles, without a lawful basis, or use it for spam, harassment, stalking, profiling, discrimination or unlawful surveillance;
- copy and republish content in a way that infringes copyright, database rights or other rights;
- request pages so fast or so often that it harms a site. PageHarvest waits at least one second between pages and stops when a site answers "Too Many Requests"; keep the wait reasonable, and do not start the job again at once;
- collect sensitive data, such as health or financial data, children's data or government identifiers, unless the law clearly allows it;
- break any law that applies to you or to the site.

The developer cannot see what you collect, but may stop distributing PageHarvest, or change it, to prevent misuse.
